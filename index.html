How To Play

WASDMove MouseLook around — click the screen to capture the cursor SpaceJump (hold at the edge for a moment of coyote time) ShiftSprint Ctrl / CCrouch — the only way through vents and low ducts ETalk, pull levers, press buttons, swipe cards, pick things up W/SClimb up and down when you're on a ladder RRespawn at the last checkpoint if you get stuck P / EscPause MSound on / off VSwitch between first and third person
Every level ends at a large door. It will not open until you've solved the room feeding it. Push crates onto pressure plates, cut the power, find the right keycard — and talk to every prisoner you meet. They know things you don't, and some of them are holding what you need.
There are Sound, View, Pause and Quit buttons in the bottom-right corner while you play. The mouse is captured during play, so press P to release the cursor before clicking them — or just use the keys.
